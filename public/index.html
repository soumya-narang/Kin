Kin

A care companion on Alexa+

Everyday conversations, turned into care the family can see.

The person you care for just talks to their speaker. Kin remembers, notices patterns over days, and tells the family when something needs a call.

The speaker

The Echo in their home, simulated in your browser. Talk to Kin by voice, the way they would.

Family view

How they're doing, what Kin noticed and why, and messages that Kin reads aloud to them.

Built on the Model Context Protocol and Amazon Bedrock. The speaker is simulated in the browser for this demo.
